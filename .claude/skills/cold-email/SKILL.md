# cold-email

Harvey's sales playbooks, used by hand. You draft, the owner sends from their
own Gmail. Nothing here sends email or collects contacts.

Read before writing:

- `revaluate180.md` - what R180 sells and the facts you may claim. Where a
  playbook says `product_knowledge.md`, it means this file.
- `email_frameworks.md` - the rules for every cold email. The most important file.
- `signal_playbook.md` - turning "why them, why today" into the first line.

Read when needed: `objection_handling.md` and `offer_strategy.md` for replies,
`lead_qualification.md` to judge if a lead is worth it, `account_navigation.md`
for who to email first at a company. Where they say Harvey, Scout or Instantly,
read "you" and "the owner's Gmail".

## Writing a sequence

1. **Get the specifics** in one batched question: the person (name, title,
   company or school), the reason to email today (a job post, news, a post
   they wrote, with a link) and the country they are in.
2. **Read any link** the owner gives and use only what it shows. The best R180
   reason is a hiring signal: a company hiring several roles or a senior leader
   fits Hiring & Selection. If there is no real reason, say so and use the
   "When you have no real signal" fallback.
3. **Write three emails** per `email_frameworks.md`: under 75, 60 and 40 words,
   one idea and one question each, lowercase subject of 2-5 words, plain text,
   no link and no product name in email 1. Every claim must be in
   `revaluate180.md` or the prospect's own public page.
4. **Footer** under each cold email (not replies): the sender's name and title,
   `Not interested? Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.`, and the postal
   address for US recipients. If a footer fact is missing, ask once.
5. **Run `li-human`** on the drafts, then show each email with its word count,
   the trigger it uses, and when to send it (day 1, day 4-6, day 10-14).

## Replies

Opt-outs ("no thanks", "stop", "remove me") get no pitch, one polite line at
most, and the owner stops emailing that person for good. Otherwise diagnose
the objection with `objection_handling.md`, keep the reply shorter than
theirs, and offer the next step from `offer_strategy.md` (the demo link
in `revaluate180.md`).

## Sending (tell the owner once)

Send by hand, about 10-20 a day. Lots of cold email from the main
revaluate180.com mailbox can land everything in spam, including normal mail.
For volume, use a separate sending domain set up for email first.
