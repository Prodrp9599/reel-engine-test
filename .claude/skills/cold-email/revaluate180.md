## What it is

A values-based people analytics platform. Headline: "Build teams that perform,
grow, and stay." Pitch: most people decisions are made on gut feelings; R180
fills the gap left by an organization's existing tools and works alongside
Hogan, Predictive Index and Culture Amp.

## What the customer gets

- A 30-minute online value assessment that maps each person across four value
  clusters: Global Vision, Regulation, Personal Empowerment and Openness.
- A plain-language Value Profile and Feedback Report for each person.
- Team analysis: value alignment, Value Blockers (unconscious biases) and lost
  values across the team.
- A debrief with an organizational psychologist for each person.
- Optional longitudinal dashboard for HR to track development over time.
- From kickoff to first insights in 14 days. Steps: Assess, Analyze, Act.
- SOC 2-aligned and GDPR-compliant practices.

## Use cases (website wording)

- **Hiring & Selection**: "Make data-backed hiring decisions. Match candidates
  to the right roles, teams, and culture before the offer goes out."
- **Team Assessment**: "Boost engagement, surface leadership potential, and
  keep your best people from walking out the door."
- **Career Transitions**: "Protect your brand, culture, and a transitioning
  employee when it's time for them to move on."
- **For Schools**: "Most students pick majors based on prestige or pressure."
  R180 helps counselors guide students toward paths that fit who they are.

## Who buys

HR and hiring leaders and team leaders, from one team making a critical hire
to enterprises rolling out across thousands of people. Schools: counselors.
